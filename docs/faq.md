No. Source: [FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq).

<br>

Cursor account (or linked / Grok Business paths in billing docs). Sources: [FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq), [get started](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/get-started).

<br>

Yes for cloud turns and routines. Source: [FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq).
