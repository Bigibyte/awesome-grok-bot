### Bots and teammates

Create `Research Bot`, remember never to send mail. Source: [overview](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview).

### Group chat

`Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+N`, add 2 to 6 Bots, assign owners with `@`. Source: [chat and collaboration](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/chat-and-collaboration).

### Skills and Teach a task

"Save as skill Weekly competitor brief" or Teach a task (up to 10 minutes). Sources: [skills and routines](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations), [overview](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview).

### Library and Pages

"Make a Page comparing these three quotes." Open from chat card or Bot name → Library. Pin rows. Share carefully (`pages.grok.bot` for public). Needs 0.60.0+ / 1.14.0+; not Enterprise. Source: [files and results](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/files-and-results).

### Computer and browser

"Screenshot this public page into `/workspace/reports/` and summarize." Shared PC. Source: [computer and apps](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/computer-and-apps).

### Routines

Weekday 09:05 Inbox skill; create Disabled, test, Enable. Sources: [skills and routines](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations), [Grok Bot for Work](https://x.ai/bot/guides/grok-bot-for-work).

### Connectors and plugins

Connect Gmail; "Draft replies, never send." Shortcut `Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+M` or `Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+W`.

### Memory

"Remember: in group chats, reply in the group."

### Voice (dictate, memos, voice chat)

Dictate `Cmd/Ctrl+D`. Play Bot voice memos. Live **voice chat** via composer button (no shortcut). One at a time; not in groups. Source: [chat and collaboration](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/chat-and-collaboration).

### Agent email and inboxes

Use `mail.grokbot.com`. Routine outbound waits as draft. Sources: [FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq), [chat and collaboration](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/chat-and-collaboration), [changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot).

### Purchases and Vault

Vault / Secure Form fill secrets by reference. Checkout may use Vault, Shop Pay, X Money where available. Saving a card in Vault can fill checkout without a separate approval each time. Sources: [security](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/security), [approvals](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/approvals-security-and-privacy), [changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot).

### Cursor Cloud Agents

Bot delegates coding; files return in chat; View PR / Open in Cursor. Admins can disable. Sources: [files and results](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/files-and-results), [teams](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/teams-and-enterprises).

### Settings

`Cmd/Ctrl+,` → General, Computer, Vault, Usage & Billing, Updates. Per-Bot: click name or `Cmd+Shift+I` (macOS). Source: [settings](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/settings-and-notifications).

### Meetings (verified slice)

Calendar EA pattern and Google Calendar connector notes exist in [Grok Bot for Work](https://x.ai/bot/guides/grok-bot-for-work) and [changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot). This page does not invent a separate join-Zoom product feature.
