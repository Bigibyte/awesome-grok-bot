Official: [approvals, security, and privacy](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/approvals-security-and-privacy) · [security](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/security).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Keep approvals on for send, post, buy, delete. Auto Review adds another layer.

- Prefer Vault, 1Password brokered login (rolling out), Secure Form
- Do not paste passwords into chat when a secure path exists
- Bots share one computer
- Cards saved in Vault may fill checkout without a separate approval each time; skip saving cards if you want to approve every payment ([security](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/security))
- Official use cases often end with "do not send" ([use cases](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/use-cases))
