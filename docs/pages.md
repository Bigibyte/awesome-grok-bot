# Pages

> [!TIP]
> If this guide helps you, please give it a [star](https://github.com/bigibyte/awesome-grok-bot) so more people can find it.

Pages are lasting outputs: reports, dashboards, and trackers that live in your Library. Source: [Pages help](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/pages).

| Card | Official help |
|------|----------------|
| <img src="https://images.bigibyte.com/projects/bigibyte-github-awesome-grok-bot-v2-card-pages-help.png" width="140" alt=""> | [cursor.com/help/grok-bot/pages](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/pages) |

> [!NOTE]
> Community and help notes mention Pages on desktop 0.60.0+ and phone 1.14.0+, and that Enterprise may not have Pages yet. Always check your build and plan.

## When to ask for a Page

- Research briefs you will revisit
- Weekly digests
- Trackers that update over time
- Comparison tables you want to share inside the product
