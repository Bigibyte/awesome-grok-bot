## What you need

- A computer or phone that Grok Bot supports: macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone (iOS 18+), or Android (9+). Source: [get started](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/get-started).
- A Cursor account (or the linked / Grok Business path described in billing docs). Source: [FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq).

## Checklist: install

- [ ] Go to [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot) or [cursor.com/download/bot](https://cursor.com/download/bot)
- [ ] Download for your platform and open the app
- [ ] Tap **Sign in** and finish with Cursor

## Checklist: your first Bot

- [ ] Meet the Bot the app shows you, or create a new named Bot
- [ ] Tell it the job in plain words
- [ ] Ask it to remember those rules
- [ ] Give a **safe first task** that changes nothing outside chat
- [ ] When it asks for access, grant only what that task needs

<details>
<summary><strong>Click: example first message</strong></summary>

<br>

```
You are my research Bot. You summarize pages and never post or send anything.
For this task: summarize this document in five bullets, list dates and open questions, do not edit the file.
```

</details>

## Connect apps later

Open **Connect apps** when you need Gmail, Slack, Notion, GitHub, or other plugins. Prefer a connector over browser login when both exist. Source: [computer and apps](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/computer-and-apps).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Read [safety-privacy.md](safety-privacy.md) before giving passwords or card details.

## After the first win

1. Save a good process as a **skill**.
2. Turn a recurring skill into a **routine**.
3. Browse [prompt-library.md](prompt-library.md).
