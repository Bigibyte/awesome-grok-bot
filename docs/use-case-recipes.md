<details open>
<summary><strong>Sales</strong></summary>

<br>

**Account brief** · Bot: Sales Prep · research company, pain hypotheses, public stakeholders · Page + optional email draft · Stop: do not contact prospects

**Outreach pack** · Draft personalized notes from a CRM export or sheet · Stop: do not send

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Recruiting</strong></summary>

<br>

**Shortlist** from a role brief and public profiles or an attached list · ranked table + outreach drafts · Stop: do not message candidates

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Marketing / paid media</strong></summary>

<br>

**Spend review** · Summarize campaign numbers from a connected sheet or export · Draft a Slack update · Stop: do not change budgets

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Ops / finance</strong></summary>

<br>

**Expense digest** · Missing receipts, duplicates, totals · Stop: do not submit or approve

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Product / engineering</strong></summary>

<br>

**Bug repro pack** · Steps, expected vs actual, severity suggestion · Stop: do not change production

**Launch checklist Page** · Owners, risks, open questions · Stop: do not page people unless you approve

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Chief of staff / founders</strong></summary>

<br>

**Daily digest** tied to 3 priorities · Calendar + named Slack channels + task file · Stop: do not message anyone for you

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Content / research</strong></summary>

<br>

**Competitor watch routine** · Weekly skill + Monday routine · Page update + Slack draft · Stop: do not auto-post

</details>
