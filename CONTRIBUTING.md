# Contributing to Awesome Grok Bot

Thanks for helping beginners learn Grok Bot.

## What we accept

- Working links to official docs, honest tutorials, videos, or community guides
- Fixes for broken links or outdated wording
- New beginner tips that match official behavior
- Clear English a middle-school student can follow

## What we reject

- Broken or unverified URLs
- Invented features, menus, prices, or click paths
- Secrets, cookies, tokens, or private account dumps
- Em dashes or en dashes in new copy (use commas, periods, or parentheses)
- Referral / affiliate sales pitch language
- Spam, ads, or off-topic lists

## How to add a resource

1. Open the URL yourself and confirm it loads.
2. Note the title, author or publisher, and what a beginner learns.
3. Add it under the right section in `README.md` or a `docs/` guide.
4. For videos, download the YouTube thumb (`i.ytimg.com` maxresdefault or hqdefault), resize compact (about 480x270), save under `assets/thumbs/`, and show it in the table.
5. For articles, add a compact dark card under `assets/thumbs/` in the same visual style as existing cards.
6. Prefer official sources when two pages say different things.
7. Open a pull request with a short note: what you checked and when.

## Style

- Simple words. Short sentences.
- No hype words like "game-changing" or "ultimate".
- Say "Bot", "skill", "routine", "cloud computer", and "Connect apps" the way the product docs do.
- Keep the footer line: "If this saved you time, a star helps others find it."

## Questions

Open an issue on this repo. For product support, use official Cursor / SpaceXSI channels, not this list.
